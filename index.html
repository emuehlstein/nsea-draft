The Weekly Radio Net

NSEA has conducted weekly radio check-ins since the late 1960s — first on CB, now on GMRS. The net keeps your equipment proven, your radio procedure sharp, and makes you known as a communications resource in time of emergency or disaster. All licensed GMRS operators are welcome and encouraged to check in.

Traffic Priorities

Emergency

An immediate threat to life or property. Supersedes all other traffic — all other units immediately stand by. “Five Two Six, EMERGENCY.”

Priority

An urgent matter needing prompt attention, but not an emergency. “Five Seven Zero, PRIORITY.”

Routine

All other traffic; yields to Emergency and Priority. No priority is stated for routine traffic.

Why NSEA?
